If you are having any of these right now

  • Trouble breathing
  • Throat tightness or swelling
  • Trouble swallowing or speaking
  • Dizziness, lightheadedness, or fainting

Use epinephrine if you have it, then call your local emergency number (911 in the United States, Canada and Mexico; 112 in most of Europe; 999 in the United Kingdom; 000 in Australia; 119 in Japan) or go to the nearest emergency facility. Do not wait.
